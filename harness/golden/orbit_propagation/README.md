## What it is

Orbit math evaluated by KSP's Orbit class (direct calls on the shipped-IL-equivalent assembly). Frame: KSP 'zup' local frame returned by getRelativePositionAtUT / getOrbitalVelocityAtUT (Planetarium.Zup.WorldToLocal applied; Zup = PlanetaryFrame(0,90,0) = identity up to ~6e-17 terms). Angles: inclination/LAN/argPe in degrees, anomalies in radians. Central bodies use in-game mu (see bodies dataset).

## Conventions

- Numbers are exact: doubles are written as the shortest of G15/G16/G17 that round-trips, floats as the shortest of G6..G9; NaN and infinities are the strings "NaN", "Infinity", "-Infinity". Columns typed `f32` hold float32 values (compare in float32 ULPs).
- Rows are identified by the table's `key` columns; compare with `python -I harness/compare/compare.py`.
- Units: SI unless stated (m, s, kg, K); KSP pressure is kPa in CelestialBody and atm in engine curves; masses in engine tables are tonnes; forces from FlightIntegrator/ModuleEngines/ModuleLiftingSurface are kN.

## Frames

- Elements: inclination, LAN, argument of periapsis in degrees; anomalies in radians; sma < 0 for hyperbolic orbits (KSP convention).
- Vectors: KSP 'zup' frame as returned by Orbit.getRelativePositionAtUT / getOrbitalVelocityAtUT (right-handed, z = orbit-normal of an equatorial prograde orbit; Unity world coordinates are the .xzy swizzle). Planetarium.Zup = PlanetaryFrame(0, 90, 0) which is the identity up to ~6e-17 cross terms.
- Solver branches: e < 0.8 Newton (|dE| <= 1e-7, unbounded iterations), 0.8 <= e < 1 eight fixed iterations of a Laguerre-type update, e >= 1 hyperbolic Newton (|dE| <= 1e-7).
