## What it is

Atmosphere and flight-state values computed by KSP's own CelestialBody and FlightIntegrator methods. FlightIntegrator is hosted without Unity (fields set: currentMainBody, altitude, spd, sunDot, atmosphereTemperatureOffset, situation) and its real protected methods CalculatePressure() and CalculateConstantsAtmosphere() are invoked; CalculateConstantsAtmosphere is called twice per row because GetSolarAtmosphericEffects reads the density left by the previous physics frame (the second call is the steady state). HighLogic.CurrentGame.Parameters = new GameParameters() (Normal difficulty, ReentryHeatScale = 1).

## Conventions

- Numbers are exact: doubles are written as the shortest of G15/G16/G17 that round-trips, floats as the shortest of G6..G9; NaN and infinities are the strings "NaN", "Infinity", "-Infinity". Columns typed `f32` hold float32 values (compare in float32 ULPs).
- Rows are identified by the table's `key` columns; compare with `python -I harness/compare/compare.py`.
- Units: SI unless stated (m, s, kg, K); KSP pressure is kPa in CelestialBody and atm in engine curves; masses in engine tables are tonnes; forces from FlightIntegrator/ModuleEngines/ModuleLiftingSurface are kN.

## Inputs

- Altitude in m above sea level; speed in m/s (air-relative); sunDot = dot(sun direction, local up); atmTempOffset = the latitude/sun temperature offset normally produced by CelestialBody.GetAtmoThermalStats (needs scene transforms; capture-spec item C4).
- Pressures in kPa (staticPressurekPa) and atm (staticPressureAtm = kPa * 0.009869232667160128).
