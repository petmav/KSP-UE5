## What it is

Every ModuleEngines/ModuleEnginesFX in the stock part cfgs. Modules are constructed with their real constructors, [KSPField]s are parsed by KSP's BaseFieldList.Load, FloatCurves by FloatCurve.Load; maxFuelFlow/minFuelFlow/throttleMin are the re-hosted OnLoad statements (ModuleEngines.cs:636-647). Thrust/Isp come from the real atmosphereCurve.Evaluate, MaxThrustOutputAtm, MaxThrustOutputVac, GetEngineThrust, getExhaustVelocity. Pressures are in atm (KSP evaluates atmosphereCurve in atm). The rocket_equation table re-hosts DeltaVStageInfo.StoreInterimDeltaV's dV expression for a single +Y engine (the stock VesselDeltaV simulation itself needs a full ShipConstruct and is capture-spec only).

## Conventions

- Numbers are exact: doubles are written as the shortest of G15/G16/G17 that round-trips, floats as the shortest of G6..G9; NaN and infinities are the strings "NaN", "Infinity", "-Infinity". Columns typed `f32` hold float32 values (compare in float32 ULPs).
- Rows are identified by the table's `key` columns; compare with `python -I harness/compare/compare.py`.
- Units: SI unless stated (m, s, kg, K); KSP pressure is kPa in CelestialBody and atm in engine curves; masses in engine tables are tonnes; forces from FlightIntegrator/ModuleEngines/ModuleLiftingSurface are kN.

## Inputs

- Engine modules from the stock part cfgs (harness/inputs). Pressure in atm. Kerbin conditions from Kerbin's real atmosphere functions. rocket_equation: masses in tonnes, thrust kN, Isp s, g0 = 9.80665.
