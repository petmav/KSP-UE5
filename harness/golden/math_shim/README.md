## What it is

Direct evaluations of managed Unity 2019.4 math (UnityEngine.CoreModule IL: Mathf/Vector3/Quaternion operators; float32) and KSP double-precision math (Vector3d, UnityEngine.QuaternionD in Assembly-CSharp, UtilMath, Planetarium.CelestialFrame). Columns a0..a5 are inputs, r0..r3 outputs; unused slots are null. Note: Mathf functions that wrap System.Math use the .NET Framework CRT here, not Unity's Mono/UCRT, so transcendental results may differ by 1 ulp from in-game values.

## Conventions

- Numbers are exact: doubles are written as the shortest of G15/G16/G17 that round-trips, floats as the shortest of G6..G9; NaN and infinities are the strings "NaN", "Infinity", "-Infinity". Columns typed `f32` hold float32 values (compare in float32 ULPs).
- Rows are identified by the table's `key` columns; compare with `python -I harness/compare/compare.py`.
- Units: SI unless stated (m, s, kg, K); KSP pressure is kPa in CelestialBody and atm in engine curves; masses in engine tables are tonnes; forces from FlightIntegrator/ModuleEngines/ModuleLiftingSurface are kN.

## Scope

- Only managed code is evaluated. Unity's native Quaternion/Vector3 functions and KSP's QuaternionD members that are declared extern in Assembly-CSharp (Euler, FromToRotation, Slerp, Lerp, AxisAngle, ToEuler) are capture-spec items (CAPTURE-SPEC.md C3); note those QuaternionD externs are not registered by Unity either.
