## What it is

Every body of the stock Kerbol system as KSP holds it at UT=0 after PSystemManager spawn, CelestialBody Awake/Start, OrbitDriver.Start and the first Planetarium.FixedUpdate/CBUpdate. Serialized inputs come from the PSystem prefab in sharedassets9.assets (see golden/serialized/celestial_bodies.serialized.json). Derived quantities: Mass/gravParameter/gMagnitudeAtCenter/SOI/hillSphere/solar-period conversion/rotation values are RE-HOSTED expressions of Awake/Start/CBUpdate (Rehosted/CelestialBodyLifecycle.cs); atmosphere constants come from the real CelestialBody.SetupConstants(); orbit quantities from the real Orbit.Init()/UpdateFromUT(0); atmosphere profiles from the real GetPressure/GetTemperature/GetDensity/GetSpeedOfSound.

## Conventions

- Numbers are exact: doubles are written as the shortest of G15/G16/G17 that round-trips, floats as the shortest of G6..G9; NaN and infinities are the strings "NaN", "Infinity", "-Infinity". Columns typed `f32` hold float32 values (compare in float32 ULPs).
- Rows are identified by the table's `key` columns; compare with `python -I harness/compare/compare.py`.
- Units: SI unless stated (m, s, kg, K); KSP pressure is kPa in CelestialBody and atm in engine curves; masses in engine tables are tonnes; forces from FlightIntegrator/ModuleEngines/ModuleLiftingSurface are kN.

## Frames and inputs

- Inputs: golden/serialized/celestial_bodies.serialized.json (extracted from sharedassets9.assets by tools/extract_assets.py).
- Orbit vectors are in KSP's 'zup' frame (Orbit.pos/vel: Planetarium.Zup.WorldToLocal of the orbital-frame vectors; Unity world = .xzy swizzle). Kerbin's OrbitDriver has reverse = true (KSP keeps Kerbin fixed and moves the Sun; irrelevant for relative orbits).
- G = 6.67408e-11, g0 = 9.80665. gravParameter is computed as (R*R*(GeeASL*g0)/G)*G, so it is not exactly R^2*g0*GeeASL.
