## What it is

Stock aerodynamics. Drag cubes are the stock PartDatabase.cfg cubes loaded by DragCubeList.LoadCubes and evaluated by the real DragCubeList.SetDragWeights / SetPartOcclusion (part without attachments) / SetDrag. 'flow_dir' is the air-relative velocity direction in part-local coordinates (Unity left-handed, +Y = part up); SetDrag is called with -flow_dir exactly as FlightIntegrator.UpdateAerodynamics passes dragVectorDirLocal. Drag/lift force scalars use the real FlightIntegrator.CalculateDragValue and the re-hosted scalar statements of UpdateAerodynamics (FlightIntegrator.cs:2353-2370). Lifting surfaces use the real ModuleLiftingSurface.GetLiftVector / GetDragVector with SetupCoefficients' vector arithmetic re-hosted (it reads a Transform).

## Conventions

- Numbers are exact: doubles are written as the shortest of G15/G16/G17 that round-trips, floats as the shortest of G6..G9; NaN and infinities are the strings "NaN", "Infinity", "-Infinity". Columns typed `f32` hold float32 values (compare in float32 ULPs).
- Rows are identified by the table's `key` columns; compare with `python -I harness/compare/compare.py`.
- Units: SI unless stated (m, s, kg, K); KSP pressure is kPa in CelestialBody and atm in engine curves; masses in engine tables are tonnes; forces from FlightIntegrator/ModuleEngines/ModuleLiftingSurface are kN.

## Frames

- Part-local Unity coordinates (left-handed, +Y = part 'up' / stack axis). Face order XP, XN, YP, YN, ZP, ZN. flow_dir = air-relative velocity direction in part space.
- In game FlightIntegrator multiplies AreaDrag by PhysicsGlobals.DragCubeMultiplier (0.1), pseudo-Reynolds multiplier, q (kPa) and DragMultiplier (8): see drag_chain_kerbin.
